Before you book

Is there a photographer on site?

No — the studio is self-service and self-operating. You bring your own photographer, or come shoot yourself.

When can I access the space?

The studio opens 10 minutes before your booking and closes 10 minutes after — booking time includes your own setup and pack-up.

How do I confirm a booking?

Proof of payment is required to confirm, made at least 24 hours ahead of your session.

What if I need to change my date?

A 1,000 KSH fee applies for date changes. Bookings are not refundable.
